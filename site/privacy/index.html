Policy · August 28, 2026

Privacy, in plain language

Caption Cues keeps caption text and settings in your browser.

What stays in your browser

The extension reads standard browser caption tracks and selected caption text shown on the page. It processes that text in your browser. Caption text, saved words, rules, text size, and caption background are not sent to Caption Cues or Sociobot.

Demo storage

The sample uses local storage keys that start with demo:. It never reads or writes extension settings. Reset demo and Start for real remove those sample keys.

Analytics and network access

This release has no analytics, ads, tracking pixels, remote fonts, or remote runtime scripts. The extension does not make external network requests.

Your control

Reset the sample from its banner. Remove extension settings by uninstalling Caption Cues or clearing its storage. Email privacy@sociobot.in with privacy questions.
