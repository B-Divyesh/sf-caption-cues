Method 02

It marks up. It doesn’t listen in.

Caption Cues works with caption text the page already exposes. It never downloads video, generates a transcript, or attempts to bypass protected streams.

  1. 01

    Turn captions on

    Start the subtitles or captions offered by your video player.

  2. 02

    Choose your cues

    Enable names, speakers, sounds, or add a word you often miss.

  3. 03

    Replay without hunting

    Press Alt + R to return to the last timed cue or reshow the last DOM caption.

Honest limitationSome protected players draw captions inside video or closed components. If the browser cannot expose that text, Caption Cues leaves it alone and tells you it is waiting.

Notes 04

Before you press play

How do I install the download?

Unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose “Load unpacked,” and select the unzipped folder. Store packaging can be added after factory release.

Does it generate or send my captions anywhere?

No. Caption processing happens in your browser. Caption text and saved words are not sent to us.

Which videos work?

Pages exposing standard browser text tracks or visible caption DOM can work. Players that hide captions in video pixels or inaccessible closed components cannot be enhanced.

Is this a hearing test or medical profile?

No. The controls are personal reading preferences, not diagnosis or medical advice.
