Terms for using Caption Cues

Use Caption Cues only with content and captions you may access.

What the extension does

Caption Cues changes how exposed caption text looks. It does not create captions, capture audio, download video, bypass protected players, diagnose hearing loss, or promise support for every player.

Using the sample

The demo contains fictional sample captions. Demo changes stay in its separate browser storage and are removed when you reset or leave the demo.

Acceptable use

Use the extension only with content and captions you may access. Do not use it to bypass technical restrictions or violate another person’s rights.

Availability and warranty

The software is provided “as is,” without warranties. Caption behavior varies across sites, and sites may change. Where law allows, Sociobot is not liable for indirect loss caused by the software.
